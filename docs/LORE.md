# Starfold: The World

[中文](LORE_zh-CN.md)

> The world was flat once. Something folded it.

## The flat world

In the beginning the world was flat.

The land spread out in every direction and did not end. Mountain followed mountain and sea followed sea, and whichever way you walked, and for however long, there was always more ahead. Above the sky there was nothing.

## The Fold

Then They came.

They came from outside the world. Gods, or a civilization of some kind: nobody can say. They left no name, no likeness and not one written word. What They left is what They did.

They cut the land apart, folded it piece by piece into planets, set the planets in the void and sent them circling stars.

Those who came after called this **the Fold**.

## The shape of a planet

A flat world cannot become a sphere. That is the deepest law of the world, and not even They got around it.

So a planet is not round. Each one is six pieces of land, folded at their edges and joined into a whole. Standing on the ground you notice nothing: it is flat underfoot, and the horizon is where it should be. Even where two pieces of land meet, you walk across and feel nothing. Only if you pick a direction and keep to it, until one day you arrive where you set out, do you understand: this land no longer runs on without end. It has been folded.

Every piece of land comes from the world that was. Everything on it is as it used to be.

## Fold points and stabilizers

There are places where the Fold does not close.

Every planet has eight corners, where three faces meet. There the structure of space cannot hold itself together. These eight places are the **fold points**.

In each fold point They set a **stabilizer**. With all eight working, the planet holds. Space around a stabilizer is unstable; that region is the **anomaly zone**. Whoever comes near is kept out, and nobody has been inside.

A stabilizer cannot be destroyed. It does one thing: it keeps the planet as it is.

## The sea of stars

Every planet in the sky was made this way. Six pieces of land, eight fold points, eight stabilizers.

Some are large and some small. Some are bare, some covered in forest, some drowned in ocean. But they have the same origin, and they were made the same way.

None of them is different from the rest. Neither is the one under your feet.

## The core

Deep inside every planet is its **core**, wrapped in a shell that cannot be broken. Dig down from the surface as far as you like; you will not reach it.

Some guess that the stabilizers are the only road to the core. Nobody has shown it.

## What nobody knows

- Who They are, and where They are now.
- Why They did it.
- How large the old world was, how many planets it became, and whether the sea of stars has an end.
- When the Fold happened.
- What is inside a core.
- Whether every planet is as quiet as it looks.

## You

You are on one of these planets.

If you want to know what the others are like, there is one way: build a ship yourself, and fly.

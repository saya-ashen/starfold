# Biotech art sources

All files in `source/` were generated on 2026-11-01 (blocks) and 2026-11-02 (Host atlas tiles, item icons) with a paid external image-generation service, then reduced by us. They are original generated images, not
copies of third-party textures. `tools/organic-textures.py` turns them into the shipped 16x textures and
the 128 px textures in `hd-pack/`.

| File | Kind | Prompt (abridged) |
|---|---|---|
| `flesh-hd.png` | 1024 px source, stored at 512 px | seamless top-down wet alien flesh, crimson muscle folds, swollen veins, moist highlights |
| `chitin-hd.png` | same | seamless dark reddish-brown chitin carapace, overlapping plates, glossy bevels, iridescent sheen |
| `resin-hd.png` | same | seamless translucent amber resin with bubbles and darker streaks over red tissue |
| `sac-hd.png` | same | seamless cluster of glowing egg sacs in dark red flesh, veined translucent membranes |
| `flesh-px20.png` | pixel-art generation sampled on its 20x20 grid | 16x16 pixel-art alien flesh, thick purple-red veins, pink highlights |
| `chitin-px24.png` | pixel-art generation sampled on its 24x24 grid | coarse pixel-art cracked beetle shell, five large irregular plates, copper edge highlights |
| `sac-px48.png` | pixel-art generation sampled on its 48x48 grid | coarse pixel-art translucent sacs with crimson rims and glowing salmon cores |
| `resin-px20.png` | pixel-art generation sampled on its 20x20 grid | 16x16 pixel-art amber resin with bubbles and streaks |
| `host-flesh.png` | pixel-art generation, stored at 512 px; Host atlas tile (32 px mod, 128 px pack) | seamless 32x32 pixel-art wet crimson muscle with fibrous striations and dark purple vessels |

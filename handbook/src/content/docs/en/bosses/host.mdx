**Habitat:** A designated nest pit on Flesh. Bring in-game armor, a ranged weapon, ammunition, food, and healing supplies. Park first and mark both the landing site and a way out of the pit. Don’t take every supply into the combat area. See Stage 6B for the approach.

<details id="encounter">
<summary>Fight and drops (spoilers)</summary>

1. **Trigger:** Follow the nest ridge to the designated large pit. The natural encounter starts nearby.
2. **Fight:** During the ground phase, identify and clear the sacs first. The Host takes less damage while any remain. Check your exit again when the phase changes before continuing the in-game fight.
3. **Drops and respawn:** Each defeat drops exactly **4 ganglia**. Each nest-bearing world tracks the Host separately. After **7 in-game days**, the Host can reappear in the nest on the same world. Save at least one ganglion for the Interstellar Drive.

1. Use an on-site survey to find oil rather than judging by the terrain. Craft a compact oil pump from iron, copper, iron bars, an empty bucket, and a furnace. Power it with solid fuel or FE.
2. Work through **Survey → Well → Pump**. Check the survey results before building a well. Start pumping only once the bore is clear and the well is complete.
3. Connect the crude output to a separate tank with room. The compact and larger extraction rigs draw from the same finite deposit. Restarting them won’t replenish it.

**Powered but not producing?** Check the survey for the current site, the well status, whether a fluid or block entity is blocking the bore, the output piping, and available tank space. If the deposit is depleted, survey a new site.

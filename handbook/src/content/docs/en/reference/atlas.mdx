Press `M` by default to open Atlas. Use the **surface map** for your base and mine, the **planetary map** to locate a landing site on a face, the **system map** to compare planets and moons, and the **interstellar map** to choose another system.

1. Add waypoints for your base, parking spot, and exit route on the surface map. To select or delete an existing waypoint, switch to the planetary globe and click its marker. While piloting, you can also use `/starfold mark add home` to mark your base and `/starfold mark list` to check it.
2. If you lose track of a ship, return to its parking waypoint and consult the ship records. Prospecting results are historical; check what remains at the site after mining.
3. Mount a Navigation Scope and take control at the Ship controller. While piloting, select a body in Atlas and choose **Set as Navigation Target**. Check the flight HUD for the target. Moving the map view closer does not set navigation.
4. At your destination system, select the specific landing planet on the system map. See the osmium round trip and interstellar expedition for examples.

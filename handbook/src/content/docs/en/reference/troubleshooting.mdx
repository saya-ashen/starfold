<a id="pressure"></a>

## Pressure is too high

**Stop going deeper and exit in your planned direction immediately.** Watch both the whole-ship estimate and scoop reading. Leave before the weakest exposed part reaches its limit. Once clear, check damage, tank levels, and propellant. [Collection HUD and pressure limits](/en/progression/deuterium/)

<a id="braking"></a>

## You can’t brake

Use speed relative to the target to see whether you’re slowing. Leave room to brake. Outside your home system, check the onboard drive and drawable deuterium. Coasting won’t stop the ship.

<a id="return-fuel"></a>

## You’re low on fuel

Park if you can do so safely, conserve what’s left, and arrange resupply. Check each tank and the fuel feed. A rescue ship outside your home system also needs interstellar propulsion.

<a id="lift-off"></a>

## The ship won’t lift

Check captured blocks, thruster direction, fuel compatibility and extraction, gyroscope power, and wet weight. Unload or refit only after parking safely.

<a id="bucket"></a>

## The Spout won’t fill a bucket

Power the Mechanical Pump and check its direction and the fluid feed. Put the empty bucket on a Depot or belt rather than right-clicking a standard tank.

<a id="navigation"></a>

## You can’t find a waypoint, target, or ship

Return to your last confirmed parking spot and check Atlas ship records. If navigation doesn’t show up, check that you’re piloting the correct ship, have a Navigation Scope, and used Set as Navigation Target.

<a id="machines"></a>

## A machine isn’t producing

Check input, power, stress, ports, pump direction, and output room. Check heat for distillation, the oxygen outlet for electrolysis, and survey results and well status for oil.

Board and right-click the **Ship controller** to take control. These are the defaults for the version covered here. If they differ in your game, check the current HUD and Controls settings. Try them during a local flight on Hearth first.

| Situation | Default controls | How to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Near-surface movement | `W/S` forward/back, `A/D` sideways, `Space/Shift` ascend/descend; hold `Ctrl` with movement for more thrust | Use short inputs in an open area and watch direction, altitude, and speed |
| Near-surface attitude | Move the mouse left or right to turn the nose; `Q/E` banks left or right, and releasing levels the ship | Watch heading and attitude change |
| Spaceflight | Mouse to turn and pitch, `Q/E` to roll; `W/S` forward/reverse thrust, `A/D` sideways, `Space/Shift` ascend/descend | Read speed and distance relative to your target; heading alone doesn’t tell you how fast you’re closing |
| Exit controls or cancel automatic arrival | `R` exits the controls; press it again if prompted. During automatic arrival, `Space + Ctrl` cancels it so you can fly away manually | Land and stop before getting off. Automatic arrival doesn’t choose a safe landing site. Stay aboard in space |
| Personal flight | `Space/Shift` ascend/descend, `Ctrl` accelerate, `Alt` fly straight, `G` brake | Read the personal flight HUD; these aren’t the Ship controller bindings |

You can tackle this route before or after collecting deuterium on Amber, depending on your equipment.

1. Use Atlas to find Flesh, Amber’s outermost moon. Pack in-game armor, a ranged weapon, ammunition, food, and healing supplies.
2. Park on clear ground and mark the ship and your way out. Follow the nest ridge into the large nest pit. Confirm your exit before approaching the Host; check off the arrival quest once you reach the pit.

<Figure image="world-flesh" width={930} height={605} alt={"In-game capture · Red ridges and uneven terrain outside the nest."} caption={"In-game capture · Red ridges and uneven terrain outside the nest."} />

<details>
<summary>Spoilers: In-game encounter and combat</summary>

Follow the nest ridge to the designated large pit. The natural encounter triggers nearby. During the ground phase, clear the sacs first; the Host takes less damage while they remain. Pick up the ganglia after the fight. See [Fight and Drops](/en/bosses/host/#encounter) to check the encounter location, phase, and drops.

<div className="hb-callout hb-callout--check">

**You’re done when** you defeat the Host and bring back its ganglia. Each defeat drops four. The Host can reappear on the same nest-bearing world after seven in-game days. Keep at least one for the Interstellar Drive.

1. Find safe ground near wood, food, and water. Make stone tools, torches, and a furnace, then build an enclosed, lit shelter and storage. Sleep in your bed and make sure there’s room to respawn beside it.
2. Mark your shelter, mine entrance, and return route in Atlas. Note landmarks you can recognize along the way. You can walk between planetary faces, but avoid the fold anomalies at the eight corners early on.
3. Gather iron, copper, coal, andesite, and zinc for the workshop. Gold and redstone are deeper underground. Hearth has no osmium or diamonds. Try FTB Ultimine on a small area first: it can mine up to 32 blocks at once and costs extra hunger and tool durability.
4. For a steadier ore supply, craft a fixed mining platform with **4 Iron Ingots, 2 Copper Ingots, an Iron Pickaxe, a Furnace, and a Lever**. Prospect at the site before mining. Use coal, charcoal, or FE, and leave output space for ore and waste rock. The platform pauses when its chunk unloads or its owner goes offline. Check the site itself rather than relying on old Atlas results.

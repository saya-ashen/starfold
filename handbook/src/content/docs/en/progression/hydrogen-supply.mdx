title: "5 · Industry & Liquid Hydrogen"
---

1. Use the osmium you brought home to start Mekanism processing and power. Supply the Electrolytic Separator with water and power, and give oxygen its own outlet. Use a chemical tube to feed hydrogen into a Rotary Condensentrator in condensing mode, then use a fluid pipe to send **liquid hydrogen** to a compatible tank. For machine setup, see the official Mekanism Wiki entries for the [Electrolytic Separator](https://wiki.aidancbrady.com/wiki/Electrolytic_Separator) and [Rotary Condensentrator](https://wiki.aidancbrady.com/wiki/Rotary_Condensentrator).
2. Produce and collect **two separate batches**. For each, check inputs, output, power, ports, operating mode, and tank levels. Confirm fuel can be drawn from the onboard tank. Budget fuel for actual ship weight and local gravity, then check off the task when done.
3. Use bauxite from Saffron to make Aluminum Alloy Hull Plating and replace hull sections in batches. Compare dry weight, wet weight, lift, and actual fuel use before and after the refit. Keep the load the same for the comparison and practice takeoff and braking again.
4. Pick a route you’ll use: more osmium, aluminum, or fluorite from Prism. Set up unloading, repairs, and compatible fuel. Make **two deliveries in the same ship**. Each time, verify the ship, raw ore count, tank levels, and conditions at departure and arrival. Stay parked and resupply if reserves are low.

<div className="hb-callout hb-callout--check">

**You’re done when** both hydrogen batches are stored, the refitted ship has flown locally, and both deliveries have been checked and logged. Then check off the route quest.

</div>

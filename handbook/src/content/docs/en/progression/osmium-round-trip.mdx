1. Use Atlas to compare Fleck and Anvil by distance, approach speed, and available prospecting results. Both are in your home system and reachable on conventional fuel. Mark your Hearth base and record your ship, destination, cargo, and fuel in each tank.
2. Budget outbound and return fuel separately. Include takeoff, transit, braking, and landing for both legs, and keep a separate landing reserve. Pack an Iron Pickaxe, food, lighting, and spare parts. While piloting, use `/starfold mark add home` to mark your base and `/starfold mark list` to verify it. Check off the planning quest when ready.
3. Brake early and pick flat ground with clearance. Once stopped, mark the ship, mine, and exit route. Mine at least **8 pieces of Raw Osmium** with an Iron Pickaxe and load them into the original ship. Recalculate takeoff and return fuel using loaded wet weight, available lift, and tank levels. If fuel is short, stay parked and resupply. Check off loading after the checks are complete.
4. Fly the original ship back to Hearth. Verify the ship, ore count, and tank levels. Unload at least **8 pieces of Raw Osmium** into base storage before taking any out to smelt. Check off unloading afterward.

<div className="hb-callout hb-callout--check">

**You’re done when** the original ship has delivered the raw ore to base storage. The homeworld landing advancement tracks only your arrival. Item quests check your inventory. Check off manual tasks such as planning, loading, and unloading after doing the work.

</div>

1. Craft a Gas Scoop with **1 Precision Mechanism, 2 Advanced Control Circuits, 1 Create Fluid Tank, and 1 Rotary Condensentrator**. Install it and an empty compatible tank on the same assembled ship. Bring conventional emergency fuel and spare parts.
2. Test braking outside Amber’s atmosphere. Confirm available thrust and pick an exit direction before entering the low-pressure outer layer. Watch the Gas Scoop pressure, the HUD’s **Whole-Ship Pressure Estimate**, tank level, and damage. Pressure on parts of the hull can be higher than at the scoop.
3. Follow your planned exit before the weakest exposed component reaches its pressure limit. Make **two separate collection runs**, leaving safely each time. For each run, record scoop pressure, peak hull exposure, tank level, damage, and remaining propellant.
4. Use a powered Mechanical Pump to feed deuterium to a Create Spout. Fill an empty bucket on a Depot or belt with **1,000 mB**. Don’t right-click a standard tank with the bucket.

| Component | Pressure threshold | Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard parts and Aluminum Alloy Hull Plating | May take damage starting at **0.5** | Checked block by block |
| Osmium Alloy Pressure Hull | Threshold of **0.85** | Protects only its own block |

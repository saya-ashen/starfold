1. Build a power network with a Water Wheel, shafts, and cogwheels. Connect a Mechanical Press and Mechanical Mixer first, then a Deployer. If machinery stalls, check wheel orientation, speed, and stress. Split the load if the network is overstressed.
2. This modpack lets you craft a Blaze Burner from 3 Iron Sheets, 1 Block of Coal, and 1 Copper Ingot. Place and fuel the burner. Use a Mechanical Mixer to combine Copper and Zinc Ingots into brass in a Basin heated by a Blaze Burner. Make Rose Quartz from redstone and glass, polish it, and combine it with an Iron Sheet for an Electron Tube. Use Deployers to apply a Cogwheel, Large Cogwheel, and Iron Nugget to a Gold Sheet in that order. Count finished Precision Mechanisms, not attempts.
3. Follow Oil Survey & Extraction to survey, build a well, and pump. Collect crude oil in a separate tank.
4. Build the tower with an empty `1×1×3` Create Fluid Tank and a Distillation Controller, then add heat. From bottom to top, connect crude input, diesel output, and gasoline output. Keep the lines separate and check Mechanical Pump power and direction.
5. Refine one batch. Confirm each product reaches its own tank and can be pumped into compatible ship storage. Then refine a second batch. Your Hearth-built ship can use diesel or gasoline. Budget test flight, return, and landing fuel separately.

**You’re done when** you can make more brass, Electron Tubes, and Precision Mechanisms, you’ve collected two separate fuel batches, and the transfer line works. If crude disappears but no fuel comes out, check the heat, plumbing, and output tanks first.

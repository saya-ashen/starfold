The Escape Velocity modpack includes a one-piece schematic for the S-01. You start survival without a shuttle. Build the S-01 to practice takeoff, landing, parking, and boarding, or design your own ship for the first-ship quest.

1. Import the single schematic using the general schematic instructions. Leave room on the hatch side in preview. After building, verify that the airstair faces out and the Ship controller and gyroscopes work.
2. Add a little fuel to a compatible onboard tank and practice takeoff, braking, and landing on Hearth. Wait for a full stop before getting off, then board again using the same airstair.
3. Away from home, park clear of fold anomalies and terrain that could block the airstair. Mark the shuttle in Atlas before walking away.

<div className="hb-callout hb-callout--check">

**You’re done when** you can park at a full stop, walk from the ground through the hatch, and take control again after boarding.

</div>

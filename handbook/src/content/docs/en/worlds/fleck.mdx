Fleck is a moon of Hearth and one option for your first osmium flight. Atlas classifies it as an asteroid. Compare the actual trip and prospecting results with Anvil before leaving. Give yourself room to brake and don’t park beside a fold anomaly. Stop on flat ground and mark the ship, mine, and exit route separately. Recalculate return fuel after loading the ore.

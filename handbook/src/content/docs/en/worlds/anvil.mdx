Anvil is a rocky planet in your home system and a candidate for your first osmium trip. Compare its current distance, approach speed, and prospecting results with Fleck before choosing. Bring an Iron Pickaxe. Slow down on approach and pick flat, unobstructed ground with room to lift off again. Check loaded wet weight, lift, and return fuel after putting the ore aboard the original ship.

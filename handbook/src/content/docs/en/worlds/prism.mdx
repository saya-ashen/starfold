Prism is a crystal moon of Amber. Mine fluorite here and use it with Advanced Control Circuits to upgrade your Navigation Scope. Crystal worlds also have quartz, amethyst, and small amounts of diamonds deep underground. Mark the ship and mine once parked, load what you need, and keep fuel for the return trip.

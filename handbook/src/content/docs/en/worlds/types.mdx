| Type | What to look for | Where to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homeworld | Land and shorelines with room for a shelter, mine entrance, and workshop | [Hearth](/en/worlds/hearth/) |
| Rocky | Exposed rock; check a landing site up close rather than judging flatness from orbit | [Anvil](/en/worlds/anvil/) |
| Asteroid | On small bodies, check the outline and surface clearance separately. Size alone doesn’t tell you the Atlas world type | [Fleck](/en/worlds/fleck/), a moon of Hearth |
| Lush | Extensive vegetation; look for open ground before landing | [Viridia](/en/worlds/viridia/) |
| Archipelago | Water separates land areas; check shorelines and clearance on the island itself | [Pelagia](/en/worlds/pelagia/) |
| Desert | Sandy ground and a source of bauxite | [Saffron](/en/worlds/saffron/) |
| Mushroom | Mushroom terrain; don’t mistake a cap for a landing site | [Haven](/en/worlds/haven/) |
| Sculk | Check the sculk-covered terrain and actual ground rather than judging clearance from a dark outline | [Hush](/en/worlds/hush/) |
| End | End-type terrain; check the edge of the actual landing area on approach | [Ivory](/en/worlds/ivory/) |
| Basalt | Basalt terrain; look for changes in elevation before landing | [Cinder](/en/worlds/cinder/) |
| Frozen | Ice and uneven terrain; bright surfaces aren’t necessarily flat | [Rime](/en/worlds/rime/) |
| Crystal | Crystal terrain; inspect the ore up close when looking for fluorite | [Prism](/en/worlds/prism/) |
| Flesh | Nest ridges and pits can cut across a walking route; plan your way out first | [Flesh](/en/worlds/flesh/) |
| Gas giant | Watch atmospheric pressure rather than looking for a landing surface | [Amber](/en/worlds/amber/) |

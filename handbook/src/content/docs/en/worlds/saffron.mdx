Saffron is a desert world. Mine bauxite here, then bring it back to your workshop to smelt into Aluminum Ingots and craft Aluminum Alloy Hull Plating for a lighter hull. Leave cargo space before departure and mark the mine and walk back to the ship once parked. Budget return fuel for your actual load.

Amber is the gas giant where you collect deuterium. Test braking and pick an exit direction outside the atmosphere, then enter the low-pressure outer layer. Check both the scoop pressure and the hull pressure estimate. Individual parts can see higher pressure, and pressure hull blocks protect only themselves. Withdraw as you approach the pressure limit of the weakest exposed part.

Amber’s four moons are Cinder, Rime, Prism, and outermost Flesh.

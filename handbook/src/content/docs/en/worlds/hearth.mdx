Hearth is your starting planet in Escape Velocity. Mark your shelter, mine entrance, workshop, and fueling station separately. Practice stopping, getting off, and reboarding near base before flying from the surface to nearby orbit and back. Hearth has the usual workshop ores but no osmium or diamonds. Compare nearby Fleck with Anvil for osmium, and verify your Hearth base marker before flying home.
